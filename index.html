Welcome to the Black Student Athlete Success Playbook

Balancing the scoreboard
and academia.

This website explores the impact of extracurricular sports on the academic success of Black students in Grades 9-12 within the Toronto District School Board (TDSB). Based on my action research project and personal experience, it highlights both the benefits of sports such as confidence, leadership, and teamwork and the challenges of balancing athletics, academics, and mental well being. Explore the findings, resources, and strategies designed to support Black student athletes both in the classroom and on the field.

About the researcher

I'm Halima.

Thank you for exploring my website. I created this site to explore the impact of extra curricular sports on the academic success of Black student Grades 9-12 within the Toronto District School Board. This website shares findings from my action research project, mostly highlighting both the benefits and challenges of balancing school work while also participating in an extra curricular sports team. From my personal experience, I know participating in sports can highlight many skills (i.e. confidence, leadership and teamwork!), but students can also face some academic challenges along the way. I understand how hard it can be to keep up with school work while trying to maintain good mental health. I built this website to help Black student athletes like myself. I believe we can balance it all and be successful!
